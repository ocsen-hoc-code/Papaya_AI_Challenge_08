## Notes

Ground truth written to `ground_truth/<stem>.json` for all 10 files. Every `fields` block has exactly the keys of `FIELD_SPECS[document_type]`, in order, and all files parse as JSON. Fields I was not sure about:
- **discharge 05 `procedures_performed`**: recorded as the surgery only. Antibiotics, pain relief and IV fluids were left out because they are treatments, not procedures. A grader may expect them listed.
- **discharge 06 `procedures_performed`**: recorded as 'ให้สารน้ำทางหลอดเลือดดำ' (IV fluid). It is the only procedure printed.
- **discharge 04/05/06 `diagnosis.primary`**: the ICD-10 code in parentheses is kept as printed. A grader that compares only the disease name would need to strip it.
- **lab 07/08 flags**: rows with no printed flag are `null`, not `normal`, per the task rule. A grader expecting `normal` for in-range rows would disagree.
- **lab 07/08 `date`**: collection date used (the report date is the same day).
- **lab 08 result strings**: decimal comma kept (`"5,6"`), not converted to a float.
- **receipt 03 `hospital_name`**: the pharmacy's printed name is recorded verbatim.
- **prescription 10 `dosage` / `frequency`**: `dosage` is the per-dose amount (`1 viên/lần`), and `frequency` keeps the printed instruction text (for example `Uống ngày 2 lần, sau ăn`). Name keeps the strength (`Augmentin 625mg`).
- **prescription 09 `dosage` / `frequency`**: `OD` and `BD` are in the Frequency column of the render, so they are frequencies. The text layer put them in other places.
